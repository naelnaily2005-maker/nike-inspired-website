About us


Nike is one of the world's leading designers, manufacturers, and marketers of athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, and accessories. Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports and officially becoming Nike in 1971, the company is named after the Greek goddess of victory, symbolizing the brand's commitment to empowering athletes to achieve greatness. With its iconic Swoosh logo and “Just Do It” slogan, Nike has become a global symbol of innovation, performance, and style.

Dedicated to pushing the boundaries of athletic performance, Nike invests heavily in research and development to create groundbreaking products for athletes of all levels. From lightweight running shoes to high-performance basketball gear, Nike constantly strives to combine cutting-edge technology with sustainable materials. Its partnerships with elite athletes, including LeBron James, Serena Williams, and Cristiano Ronaldo, have inspired millions worldwide to pursue their passions.

Beyond sportswear, Nike is committed to making a positive impact on society and the environment. The company actively promotes sustainability through initiatives like Move to Zero, aiming for zero carbon emissions and zero waste. Additionally, Nike invests in community programs to promote physical activity and equality, ensuring that the brand stands for more than just sports—it stands for progress and empowerment for all.





Our History


Nike began its journey in 1964 when Bill Bowerman, a track and field coach at the University of Oregon, and his former student Phil Knight founded Blue Ribbon Sports. Initially, the company served as a distributor for Japanese running shoes from Onitsuka Tiger (now ASICS). However, in 1971, the founders decided to create their own line of athletic footwear, marking the birth of the Nike brand. The name "Nike" was inspired by the Greek goddess of victory, reflecting their ambition to dominate the sportswear industry.

The company’s first major breakthrough came in 1972 with the release of the Nike Cortez, a revolutionary running shoe designed for comfort and performance. In the years that followed, Nike gained widespread recognition for its innovative designs and marketing strategies. The introduction of the iconic Swoosh logo, designed by graphic design student Carolyn Davidson for just $35, and the "Just Do It" slogan in 1988 propelled Nike to global fame, making it a household name.

Throughout its history, Nike has consistently redefined sportswear by incorporating cutting-edge technology into its products. From developing the Air Max cushioning system in 1987 to the Flyknit material in 2012, Nike has remained at the forefront of innovation. These advancements, coupled with endorsements from legendary athletes such as Michael Jordan, Tiger Woods, and Kobe Bryant, have solidified Nike's reputation as a leader in the world of sports and lifestyle apparel.
